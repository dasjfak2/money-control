--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS ADDED
--------------------------------------------------------------------
1) TWO DAILY REMINDERS — morning and night — nudging you to log that
   day's income/expenses. Set in Account & Backup -> Notifications &
   Sound -> "Daily add-transaction reminder":
     - On/off switch (on by default)
     - Morning reminder time (default 09:00)
     - Night reminder time (default 21:00 — set this at least a few
       hours before your actual bedtime; the app has no way to know
       your real sleep schedule, so this is a sensible starting point
       you should adjust to fit your own routine)

2) "SMART" = ONLY REMINDS WHEN YOU ACTUALLY NEED IT — before either
   reminder fires, the app checks whether you've already added any
   transaction (income or expense) today. If you have, NEITHER
   reminder fires — logging once satisfies the whole day. If you
   open the app for the first time already past your night time
   (say, 10:30pm) having logged nothing, you get just ONE reminder
   (the night one), not a redundant morning + night pair back to back.

3) TWO WAYS IT SHOWS UP:
     - The same OS notification + sound path bills already use (needs
       notification permission + the app open/installed/backgrounded).
     - NEW: a small, dismissible in-app card that slides up from the
       bottom — doesn't block anything you're doing, has one-tap
       "− Expense" / "+ Income" buttons that jump straight to the add-
       transaction screen, an explicit close (×) button, and auto-
       dismisses itself after 15 seconds if you ignore it.
   Message text is friendly and different for morning ("Good
   morning ☀️ ... before the day gets busy") vs. night ("Before you
   sleep 🌙 ... so tomorrow's numbers start accurate").

4) A 6TH RINGTONE, "GENTLE WAVE" — a slower, warmer, descending
   3-note pattern, deliberately different in shape and pitch from
   "Soft Chime" (the bill/goal/debt/future default) so the two are
   tellable apart by ear without looking at the screen. It's the
   default for this new daily reminder specifically so it's a
   distinct sound from bill alerts, and it's just as calm/quiet as
   the rest of the ringtone set. You can pick any of the 6 ringtones
   for it independently of the bills one, and set its own "plays for"
   duration too — same dropdown pattern as the bills section.

5) CLEANUP — removed the old beep() function, which had been fully
   superseded by the new ringtone engine (playTone()/playRingtone())
   added last update and was no longer called from anywhere.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT'S BUILT (why it's safe)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
No new external domains, files, or endpoints of any kind. "Gentle
Wave" is generated the same way the other five ringtones are — plain
Web Audio oscillator notes, nothing downloaded. The in-app card is
plain HTML/CSS/JS reusing the app's existing design tokens (colors,
radii, shadows) — no new libraries, no new dependencies.
