v128 - Calculators: only what you pick, only your country
1. Tapping a calculator in More now opens ONLY that calculator. The tile grid with every other calculator is hidden, and the page title is the calculator's own name (e.g. "Income Tax Calculator").
2. "Show calculators for other countries" is no longer remembered. It used to be saved on the phone, so one accidental tap left UK/Australia/Canada/Singapore/Malaysia visible forever. It now lives in More under the Calc list and resets every time the app opens. Default: only your phone country code's calculators (+880: BD tax + Zakat; +44: UK; +1: US + Canada ...), plus Loan, Deposit and Gulf End-of-Service (always shown).
3. Country calculator rows in More now use the same font/size as Loan/Deposit rows.
Tests: v128_test.mjs (new, 18 checks) + all earlier suites pass.
