v130 — Settings / Data & reports / Suggestions & reports (2026-11-04)

What changed
- The old "Account & Backup" screen is now two separate menus in More:
  * Settings: sync/account, profile, language, privacy, notifications & sounds, reminders, app lock, updates.
  * Data & reports: storage, export/reports, backup & restore, legal, danger zone.
- New menu "Suggestions & reports": pick Suggestion / Bug report / Other, write a message, send by email
  (opens the user's mail app, addressed to the existing public contact address) or Copy.
  Optional app info (version, language, device, screen). No money data or account names are ever included.
  No backend, nothing stored or collected by the app.
- All 10 languages translated. Global search lands in the correct view. Privacy text mentions the email feedback.
- Removed the old in-page tab bar (replaced by the two menu rows).
