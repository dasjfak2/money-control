FEXAFY — STORAGE: BANGLA TEXT + FREE-UP-SPACE BUTTONS (2026-10-14, v102)
=====================================================================
Files: index.html, sw.js (v101 -> v102, versions match). Backend untouched.

1) TRANSLATION
- All storage texts (red "Storage full" banner, amber "almost full" banner,
  "Storage on this device" card, toasts, confirm questions) now go through
  the app's t() translation system.
- English + Bangla written. Other languages show English for these lines
  (the app's normal fallback) until translations are added.
- Fixed a small display bug from v101: the card tip showed a literal
  "—" instead of a dash.
- Switching language now refreshes the storage meter text too.

2) FREE UP SPACE (Account & Backup -> Storage on this device)
- "Shrink receipt photos": re-saves every receipt photo smaller (max 640px,
  JPEG 50%). Only replaces a photo if it becomes at least 10% smaller; a
  photo that fails to load is left untouched. Asks first, then shows how much
  was freed.
- "Remove receipt photos older than 1 year": deletes only the photo of
  entries dated more than 1 year ago. The entries themselves stay. Asks first
  with the exact count.
- The card also shows how many receipt photos are stored.
- Both go through the normal save(); nothing else in the data logic changed.
- Dark mode button text fixed (was dark on dark).

TESTED (real Chromium, light/dark/Bangla)
- 5 receipt photos seeded with local login: storage 2.3 MB -> 0.8 MB after
  shrink, all 5 kept; remove-old deleted 3 photos (2024/early-2025), kept the
  2 recent ones, and all 5 entries stayed. Bangla and English texts correct.
- 6 script blocks pass node --check; no duplicate names; tags balanced;
  Monthly Review and restyle still present.
LIMITS
- Both actions cannot be undone (the confirm says so and suggests a backup).
  Shrinking lowers photo quality; text on very small receipts may be harder
  to read.
- Not tested on a real phone. Other languages still English for these lines.
