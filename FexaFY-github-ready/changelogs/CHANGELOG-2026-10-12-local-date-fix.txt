FEXAFY - LOCAL-DATE BUG FIX + BENGALI CHECK (2026-10-12, v96)
==============================================================
Files touched: index.html, sw.js (v95 -> v96). Nothing else.

THE BUG
Several places turned a LOCAL-midnight date into text with toISOString(),
which converts to UTC first. In any timezone ahead of UTC (Bangladesh is
UTC+6) that gives the PREVIOUS day. Confirmed with the real extracted
functions under TZ=Asia/Dhaka before fixing:
  - addOneMonthClampDay("2026-09-23")  gave 2026-10-22  (should be 10-23)
  - Jan 31 + 1 month gave Feb 27 (should be Feb 28)
  - a paid bill rolling to its next cycle ended one day early
    (2026-11-04 instead of 2026-11-05)
  - a recurring item set to day 1 was logged on the LAST DAY OF THE PREVIOUS
    MONTH (2026-09-30 instead of 2026-10-01), so it landed in the wrong month
  - Analytics "Goals accumulation" used 30 Oct as end of October, so anything
    logged on the last day of a month was missing from that month's total
  - between midnight and 6am, "today" was yesterday: the default date in the
    Add Expense/Income form, and the daily-reminder "already logged today"
    check

THE FIX
All of these now use the app's existing localDateStr() (local y/m/d). Changed:
addOneMonthClampDay, payBillFromTx, advanceBillCycles, processRecurring,
the goals-accumulation month end, the "behind" goal suggested date,
todayStr(), and the date default in the two transaction forms.
9 lines changed, nothing added or removed.

VERIFIED
- Real extracted functions run in 5 timezones (Dhaka, UTC, New York,
  Auckland, Honolulu): all give the correct dates, including Jan 31 -> Feb 28,
  leap-year Jan 31 -> Feb 29, Dec -> Jan year rollover.
- todayStr() at 02:00 Dhaka returns the local date; bill roll-forward correct.
- 6 inline script blocks pass node --check; no duplicate functions.

NOT FIXED (needs your decision)
- Entries already saved with the wrong date are not changed. If a recurring
  item on day 1 was auto-logged before this update, it may sit on the last day
  of the previous month; edit it by hand if it matters.

BENGALI CHECK
- Home, Transactions, Bills, Analytics, Goals and Account & Backup were
  screenshotted in Bengali, light and dark: no broken letters, no overflow,
  gradient titles render correctly. Some labels are still English because
  they were never translated (not related to this update).

DEPLOYMENT
Upload index.html and sw.js. No backend change.
