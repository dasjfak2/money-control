v146 — Negative-account hint + "not enough balance" transfer pop-up (2026-11-17)

1) Hint on Transactions -> Accounts: when an account (except Borrow) is below zero, a short line explains
   it usually means money was moved in from another account without a Transfer, with an "Add transfer"
   button that opens the normal Transfer form pre-filled (richest other account -> the negative one, missing amount).

2) Pop-up before saving a NEW expense: if the paying account can't cover it, a pop-up (on top of the open form,
   nothing typed is lost) offers "Transfer <missing amount> & save" from a chosen account (Bank / other accounts /
   Borrow), "Save without transfer", or "Cancel". The transfer is a normal Transfer entry, so balances and totals
   stay correct (a Transfer is never income or expense). Not shown when: the balance is enough, the expense is paid
   from Borrow (borrowing by design), there is only one account, or an existing expense is being edited.

3) Borrow as a source: a Transfer out of Borrow now adds that amount to the "Borrowed money" debt
   (edit/delete of that transfer keeps the debt in step). Only transfers created from v146 on are affected;
   older transfers are never changed retroactively. Transfers INTO Borrow do not reduce the debt (repay on the Debt screen, as before).
