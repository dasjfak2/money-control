Privacy Policy — FexaFY

No analytics · No ad trackers
Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. What this app is

FexaFY is a personal finance and budget tracker that runs entirely in your browser (a client-only "Progressive Web App"). There is no company backend server for your financial data — your entries live on your own device, and are optionally backed up to your own Google Drive if you choose to sign in with Google.

2. Data this app stores, and where

DataWhere it's stored
Transactions, bills, debts, budgets, goals, net worth entries, receipt photosOn your device only, in the browser's local storage — encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM) with a key generated on and never leaving your device. Optionally also in a private, app-only area of your own Google Drive ("appDataFolder"), if you sign in with Google.
Profile (name, phone number, email)Same as above — local device storage, and your own Google Drive if signed in.
App Lock PINNever stored as the PIN itself — only a salted SHA-256 hash of it, on your device.
Fingerprint / face data (if you enable biometric unlock)Never touches this app or any server — handled entirely by your device's own operating system via WebAuthn.
Language, currency, theme preferenceOn your device only.

None of this data is sent to us, sold, or shared with advertisers, because there is no "us" collecting it — the app has no backend server of its own. The only place your data ever travels to is your own Google Drive, and only if and when you choose to sign in with Google.

3. No analytics, no advertising

This app does not use any analytics SDK (e.g. Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Meta Pixel), does not use any advertising SDK or ad network, and does not contain any hidden trackers. Nothing about how you use this app is measured, profiled, or monetized. If that ever changes in a future version, this section will be updated to name the tool and explain exactly what it collects — you don't need to hunt through settings to find out.

The app is mostly offline. It only contacts the following outside services, and only for the specific features named below:

No transactions, balances, accounts, or profile data are included. This only ever reaches a Vercel project and Upstash database you create and control — never the app's original author.

5. Your control over your data

  • Export a full backup (JSON) or a per-period Excel/PDF report at any time from Account & Backup.
  • Delete everything on this device at any time from Account & Backup → Reset All Data.
  • Sign out at any time; your local data stays on the device unless you also reset it.
  • Revoke this app's access to your Google Drive at any time from your Google Account's "Third-party access" settings.

6. Children's privacy

This app is a general-purpose personal finance tool and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect data from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If this app's data practices change, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change accordingly.
