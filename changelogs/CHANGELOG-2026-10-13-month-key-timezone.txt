FEXAFY - MONTH/DATE FIX FOR AMERICAS TIMEZONES + DARK ICON (2026-10-13, v99)
============================================================================
Files touched: index.html, sw.js (v98 -> v99). Nothing else.

THE BUG
monthKey() and fmtDate() turned a date-only text such as "2026-10-01" into a
Date, which JavaScript reads as midnight UTC. In any timezone BEHIND UTC (US,
Canada, ...) that is the previous evening, so:
  - an entry dated the 1st of a month was counted in the PREVIOUS month
    (Home "Spent"/"Left to spend", stat tiles, analytics, trends, budgets)
  - dates were shown one day early (Oct 1 shown as Sep 30)
Bangladesh (UTC+6) and anywhere ahead of UTC were never affected, which is
why it didn't show up here.

THE FIX
- monthKey(): a date-only text ("YYYY-MM-DD" or "YYYY-MM") is now read
  straight from the text. Dates, timestamps and text containing a time go
  through new Date() exactly as before.
- fmtDate(): a "YYYY-MM-DD" text is built as a local date before formatting.
Result: unchanged in Bangladesh/UTC and ahead; corrected in the Americas.

NOT CHANGED (on purpose)
- "Days until due" counters (bills, debts, future expenses) still compare
  midnight UTC with the current time. Changing them would shift results by
  hours for everyone, so they were left alone; in the Americas they can be
  off by one day around midnight.

DARK MODE
- The clock/calendar icon inside time and date fields (e.g. Reminder time)
  was dark on a dark field and invisible; it is now light.

VERIFIED
- Real extracted functions compared old vs new in Asia/Dhaka, America/New_York
  and Pacific/Honolulu over 8 inputs: Dhaka identical on every input; New York
  and Honolulu corrected for 1st-of-month and last-day dates.
- Real app in headless Chromium with the timezone set: an expense dated the 1st
  of this month showed Spent $0 in New York with v98 and $300 with v99; Dhaka
  showed $300 in both.
- 6 inline script blocks pass node --check; no duplicate functions.
- Dark Account page screenshot: clock icon visible. No page errors.
- Not tested on a real phone.

DEPLOYMENT
Upload index.html and sw.js. No backend change.
